Genshin Impact DPS rankings

A character-by-character comparison of damage output, based on controlled dummy testing. Each character is ranked by the strongest team tested for them.

About this ranking

What the numbers mean and how the tiers are set.

Method

  • Damage is measured in thousands of DPS on a target dummy.
  • Figures come from the end of a rotation, after 3 to 6 rotations.
  • Each team gets one R1 weapon or one C1 constellation, preferably on the main DPS.
  • Asterisked characters use math to account for a free C1.

Tier cutoffs

Terms

  • DPS: damage per second over the rotation.
  • C0 to C6: constellation level, the number of upgrades owned.
  • R1 to R5: weapon refinement level.
  • Main DPS: the character the team is built around, listed first.
  • Untested: no team has been recorded yet.
